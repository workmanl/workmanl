# Apps by Emrld Labs

Independent iPhone, iPad and Mac apps, plus a set of free tools that run in the browser. Made by Scott Manley.

## iPhone and iPad

- [CineGrade Pro](https://cinegradecolor.com): cinematic color grading for video on iPhone.
- [Image Studio Pro](https://imagestudioproapp.com): photo editing with AI styles, no subscription.
- [Frame Exporter Pro](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/frame-exporter-pro/): frame-accurate stills pulled from any video.
- [Arrangr](https://emrldlabs.com/arrangr/): photo collages and seamless Instagram carousels.
- [Whoosh Designer](https://whooshdesigner.com): whoosh and transition sound design.
- [Braam Designer](https://braamdesigner.com): cinematic bass impacts, built from scratch.
- [Tone Gen Studio](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/tone-gen-studio/): tone generator with sweeps, tuner, spectrum analyzer and metronome.
- [3D Scan Pro](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/3d-scan-pro/): LiDAR and photo scanning with mesh repair for 3D printing.
- [PreFlight](https://dronechecklist.app): a pre-flight checklist for your drone.
- [Tumbler Timer Pro](https://tumblertimerpro.com): a rock tumbling companion with batch tracking.
- [Vision Quest Games](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/vision-quest-games/): six games for kids built around visual tracking, color and memory.
- [Quick Cut Pro](https://quickcutpro.app): pick a batch of clips and get a finished cut.
- [Tank Battle Wars](https://workmanl.github.io/Tank-Battle-Wars/): turn-based artillery duels with wind and destructible terrain.
- [Jaspr](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/jaspr/): a private AI assistant that runs on your iPhone.

## Mac

- [Photo Cull Pro](https://photocullpro.com): photo culling for wedding and event photographers.
- [HDR Forge](https://hdrforgeapp.com): HDR merging for real estate and architectural photos.
- [MeshRepair](https://emrldlabs.com/apps/meshrepair/): diagnose and repair STL and OBJ files for 3D printing.

## Free web tools

These run in the browser. Nothing to install.

- [Tone Generator](https://workmanl.github.io/ToneGenerator/): tones, sweeps, tuner and RTA for audio testing.
- [Simple Synth](https://workmanl.github.io/SimpleSynth/): a lightweight browser synthesizer.
- [Cine SFX Studio](https://workmanl.github.io/Cine-SFX-Studio/): cinematic whooshes and designed effects.
- [Braam Designer (web)](https://workmanl.github.io/braam-designer/): braam and trailer-impact sound design.
- [Whoosh Designer (web)](https://workmanl.github.io/whoosh-designer/): whoosh and motion-sweep sound design.
- [Image Studio (web)](https://workmanl.github.io/Image-Studio/): App Store screenshot and device-frame studio.
- [Frame Exporter (web)](https://workmanl.github.io/Frame-Exporter-Pro/): pull frame-accurate stills from a video.
- [Vision Quest web games](https://workmanl.github.io/vision-games/): Memory Master, Space Explorer, Treasure Maze, Balloon Adventure and Nitro Snail Runner.
- [Music Discovery Map](https://musicdiscoverymap.web.app): explore music by connection.
- [AudioTrim](https://work.scottmanley.com/tool.html?t=audiotrim): a quick browser audio trimmer.
- [Audio Recorder](https://workmanl.github.io/AudioRecorder/): an in-browser audio recorder with a live level meter.
- [Sight Words Adventure](https://work.scottmanley.com/tool.html?t=sight-words-adventure): speech-guided sight-word practice for early readers.

More work: [work.scottmanley.com](https://work.scottmanley.com/creative-tools.html)
